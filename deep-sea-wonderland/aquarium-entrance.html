深海奇境 / Deep Sea Wonderland

Descend into the mysteries of the deep. An immersive virtual aquarium spanning from sunlit coral reefs to the darkest ocean trenches.
潜入深海的神秘世界。一个沉浸式虚拟水族馆，从阳光照耀的珊瑚礁延伸到最黑暗的海沟。

Exhibition Map / 展览地图

Coral Garden / 珊瑚花园

Vibrant reef ecosystems teeming with colorful corals, clownfish, and sea anemones. / 充满活力的珊瑚礁生态系统，栖息着五彩缤纷的珊瑚、小丑鱼和海葵。

Abyss Zone / 深渊区

Plunge into eternal darkness where bioluminescent creatures light up the deep. / 坠入永恒的黑暗，在这里生物发光生物点亮了深海。

Polar Waters / 极地水域

Icy realms of penguins, leopard seals, and the ethereal beauty of frozen seas. / 冰封的世界，有企鹅、豹海豹和冰冻海洋的空灵之美。

Tropical Lagoon / 热带泻湖

Warm shallow waters with rays, seahorses, and gentle sea turtles. / 温暖的浅水区，有鳐鱼、海马和温和的海龟。

Freshwater Basin / 淡水盆地

Rivers and lakes featuring arapaima, piranhas, and Amazonian biodiversity. / 河流与湖泊，展示巨骨舌鱼、食人鱼和亚马逊生物多样性。

Predator Passage / 捕食者通道

Meet the ocean's apex hunters: sharks, orcas, barracuda, and moray eels. / 邂逅海洋顶级猎手：鲨鱼、虎鲸、梭鱼和海鳝。

Medusa Gallery / 水母馆

Drift among moon jellies, lion's mane, and other hypnotic jellyfish species. / 在月亮水母、狮鬃水母和其他迷人的水母群中漂游。

Turtle Sanctuary / 海龟保护区

Learn about sea turtle conservation and watch these gentle giants glide. / 了解海龟保护，观赏这些温柔的巨型生物悠然滑行。

Creature of the Day / 今日生物

Giant Oceanic Manta Ray / 巨型海洋蝠鲼

Mobula birostris

The largest ray in the ocean, with a wingspan of up to 7 meters (23 feet). These gentle giants glide through tropical and subtropical waters, filtering plankton with their cephalic fins. Highly intelligent, they have the largest brain-to-body ratio of any fish.
海洋中最大的鳐鱼，翼展可达7米（23英尺）。这些温柔的巨型生物在热带和亚热带水域滑行，用头鳍过滤浮游生物。它们智力极高，拥有所有鱼类中最大的脑身比例。

Today's Events & Programs / 今日活动与节目

🦈 Shark Feeding Tour / 鲨鱼喂食之旅

Join our marine biologists at the Predator Passage for an up-close feeding demonstration. Learn about shark diets, hunting behaviors, and the crucial role these apex predators play in ocean ecosystems.
与我们的海洋生物学家一起在捕食者通道近距离观看喂食演示。了解鲨鱼的饮食、狩猎行为以及这些顶级捕食者在海洋生态系统中扮演的关键角色。

⏰ 10:30 AM & 3:00 PM | Duration: 45 min / 时长：45分钟

🌊 Jellyfish Encounters / 水母邂逅

Step into the ethereal world of drifting jellies. Our aquarists demonstrate jellyfish husbandry, explain their unique life cycles, and showcase newborn ephyrae under magnification.
踏入漂流水母的空灵世界。我们的水族师展示水母养殖技术，解释它们独特的生命周期，并在放大镜下展示新生水螅体。

⏰ 11:15 AM & 1:30 PM | Duration: 30 min / 时长：30分钟

🐢 Turtle Hatchling Release / 海龟幼崽放归

Witness the magical moment as rehabilitated sea turtle hatchlings make their dash to the water. Learn about our rescue program and how we protect nesting beaches worldwide.
见证被救助的海龟幼崽冲向水中的神奇时刻。了解我们的救助项目以及我们如何保护世界各地的筑巢海滩。

⏰ 4:30 PM (Seasonal) / 季节性 | Duration: 60 min / 时长：60分钟

🔬 Junior Marine Biologist Lab / 少年海洋生物学家实验室

Kids aged 6-14 can become junior scientists! Examine plankton through microscopes, touch safe tidal pool creatures, and conduct simple water quality experiments.
6-14岁的孩子可以成为少年科学家！通过显微镜观察浮游生物，触摸安全的潮池生物，并进行简单的水质实验。

⏰ 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM | Ongoing Sessions / 持续进行

🎬 Ocean Documentary Theater / 海洋纪录片影院

Immerse yourself in breathtaking underwater cinematography. Today's feature: "The Great Barrier Reef – A Living Treasure." Narrated by renowned naturalists.
沉浸在令人叹为观止的水下摄影中。今日影片：《大堡礁——一个活着的宝藏》。由著名自然学家讲述。

⏰ Every Hour on the Hour | Duration: 40 min / 每小时整点放映 | 时长：40分钟

🐙 Meet the Octopus Keeper / 与章鱼饲养员见面

Our giant Pacific octopus keepers share stories of octopus intelligence, problem-solving abilities, and the enrichment activities that keep these clever cephalopods engaged.
我们的巨型太平洋章鱼饲养员分享关于章鱼智力、解决问题能力以及让这些聪明的头足类动物保持活跃的丰容活动的故事。

⏰ 12:00 PM & 5:00 PM | Duration: 30 min / 时长：30分钟

Visitor Tips & Accessibility / 参观贴士与无障碍设施

♿ Accessibility / 无障碍设施

All exhibits are wheelchair accessible with ramps and elevators throughout. Wheelchairs available for loan at no charge. Tactile exhibits and audio descriptions available for visually impaired visitors. Service animals welcome.
所有展区均设有坡道和电梯，轮椅可通行。免费提供轮椅借用。为视障游客提供触觉展品和音频描述。欢迎服务动物。

📸 Photography Guidelines / 摄影指南

Photography is encouraged! Please disable flash in all exhibits as it can disturb marine life. Tripods and selfie sticks are not permitted in crowded areas. Tag us @DeepSeaWonderland for a chance to be featured.
鼓励拍照！请在所有展区关闭闪光灯，因为闪光灯会干扰海洋生物。拥挤区域不允许使用三脚架和自拍杆。标记我们 @DeepSeaWonderland 有机会被推荐。

🍽 Dining & Refreshments / 餐饮与小食

The Reef Cafe offers ocean-view dining with sustainable seafood options, vegetarian and vegan choices, and kid-friendly meals. Water fountains and snack kiosks located throughout the facility.
礁石咖啡馆提供海景餐饮，有可持续海鲜选择、素食和纯素选项以及儿童友好餐点。水站和小吃亭遍布整个场馆。

🅿 Parking & Transport / 停车与交通

Free parking for 500+ vehicles with EV charging stations. Public transit: Bus routes 22 and 45 stop at Ocean Blvd. Bicycle racks available at both entrances. Ride-share drop-off zone at the main gate.
超过500个车位的免费停车场，配有电动车充电站。公共交通：22路和45路公交车在海洋大道停靠。两个入口处均设有自行车架。主入口设有网约车下客区。
