Predator Passage / 掠食者通道

Where the Ocean's Apex Hunters Roam / 海洋顶级掠食者游弋之处

Enter the domain of sharks, orcas, barracuda, and moray eels — the ocean's most formidable hunters. Discover the truth behind the myths and learn how these magnificent creatures keep our oceans in balance.

进入鲨鱼、虎鲸、梭鱼和海鳗的领地——海洋中最令人生畏的猎手。揭开神话背后的真相，了解这些壮丽生灵如何维持海洋生态平衡。

Shark Species Gallery / 鲨鱼物种图鉴

Great White Shark / 大白鲨

The ocean's most iconic predator. Reaching 6m in length, with 300 serrated teeth arranged in rows. Warm-blooded regions allow it to hunt seals with explosive bursts up to 40 km/h.

海洋中最具标志性的掠食者。体长可达6米，拥有300颗锯齿状牙齿排列成行。温血身体区域使其能以高达40公里/小时的爆发速度捕猎海豹。

Apex Predator / 顶级掠食者

Hammerhead Shark / 锤头鲨

Its distinctive cephalofoil head houses electroreceptors called ampullae of Lorenzini, allowing it to detect prey buried in sand. Often seen in schools during migration.

其独特的锤形头部内有名为洛伦兹壶腹的电感受器，使其能够探测埋在沙中的猎物。迁徙时常以群体出现。

Electro-sense / 电感知

Tiger Shark / 虎鲨

Known as the "garbage can of the sea" for its indiscriminate diet. Named for dark vertical stripes that fade with age. A near-apex predator found in tropical waters worldwide.

因其无所不吃的食性而被称为"海洋垃圾桶"。因身上随年龄增长而消退的深色竖纹而得名。广泛分布于全球热带海域的近顶级掠食者。

Opportunistic Hunter / 机会主义猎手

Whale Shark / 鲸鲨

The largest fish in the ocean — up to 18m long. A gentle filter-feeder that consumes plankton, krill, and small fish. Each individual has a unique spot pattern like a fingerprint.

海洋中最大的鱼类——体长可达18米。温和的滤食性动物，以浮游生物、磷虾和小鱼为食。每条鲸鲨都有独一无二的斑点图案，如同指纹。

Gentle Giant / 温柔巨兽

Shortfin Mako / 尖吻鲭鲨

The fastest shark species, capable of bursts up to 74 km/h. Endothermic physiology keeps its body warmer than surrounding water, giving it exceptional speed and hunting prowess.

速度最快的鲨鱼物种，冲刺速度可达74公里/小时。其内温性生理机制使体温高于周围水温，赋予了它非凡的速度和捕猎能力。

Speed Demon / 速度之王

Shark Anatomy: 450 Million Years of Evolution / 鲨鱼解剖学：4.5亿年的进化

Ampullae of Lorenzini / 洛伦兹壶腹
Gel-filled pores on the snout that detect electrical fields as faint as 5 billionths of a volt — allowing sharks to locate prey hidden in sand.
吻部充满凝胶的毛孔，可探测低至50亿分之一伏特的电场——使鲨鱼能够定位藏在沙中的猎物。
Dermal Denticles / 真皮细齿
V-shaped tooth-like scales covering the skin, reducing hydrodynamic drag by up to 8%. Inspired Olympic swimsuit design.
覆盖皮肤的V形齿状鳞片，可减少高达8%的水动力阻力。曾启发奥运泳衣设计。
Cartilaginous Skeleton / 软骨骨骼
Unlike bony fish, sharks have skeletons made of cartilage — lighter, more flexible, and requiring less energy to maintain.
与硬骨鱼不同，鲨鱼的骨骼由软骨构成——更轻、更灵活，且维持所需能量更少。
Continuous Tooth Replacement / 持续换牙
Sharks can lose and replace up to 30,000 teeth in a lifetime. New teeth rotate forward on a conveyor-belt-like system.
鲨鱼一生可脱落并更换多达30000颗牙齿。新牙像传送带一样向前轮换。
Lateral Line System / 侧线系统
A sensory organ running along each side, detecting vibrations and movement in water — even in complete darkness.
沿身体两侧延伸的感觉器官，即使在完全黑暗中也能探测水中的振动和运动。

Orca: The Wolf of the Sea / 虎鲸：海洋之狼

Orcinus orca — not a whale, but the largest dolphin species / 虎鲸不是鲸鱼，而是体型最大的海豚物种

Pod Hunting Strategies / 群体狩猎策略

Orcas live in matrilineal pods of 5-30 individuals. Different ecotypes have developed specialized hunting techniques passed down through generations as cultural knowledge.

虎鲸生活在5-30只个体的母系群体中。不同的生态型形成了专门的捕猎技巧，作为文化知识代代相传。

Wave Washing / 波浪冲刷

Pods create waves to wash seals off ice floes in Antarctica. / 虎鲸群制造波浪将海豹从南极浮冰上冲下来。

Carousel Feeding / 旋转捕食

Encircling herring schools, then slapping with tail flukes to stun prey. / 包围鲱鱼群，然后用尾鳍拍击使猎物昏迷。

Beaching / 冲滩捕猎

Deliberately stranding on beaches to catch seal pups, then wiggling back into the water. / 故意冲上沙滩捕捉海豹幼崽，然后扭动身体滑回水中。

Shark Hunting / 猎鲨

Tonic immobility — flipping sharks upside down to induce paralysis before feeding on their livers. / 强直静止——将鲨鱼翻转到腹部朝上使其麻痹，然后取食其肝脏。

Other Apex Predators / 其他顶级掠食者

Great Barracuda / 大梭鱼

Reaching 1.8m, the barracuda is a lightning-fast ambush predator. Its reflective silver body confuses prey, while razor-sharp teeth and bursts of 43 km/h make escape nearly impossible.

体长可达1.8米，梭鱼是闪电般迅速的伏击型掠食者。其银光闪闪的身体迷惑猎物，锋利的牙齿和43公里/小时的冲刺速度使逃脱几乎不可能。

Ambush Striker / 伏击猎手

Giant Moray Eel / 巨海鳝

Lurking in reef crevices, the moray has a second set of jaws — pharyngeal jaws — that launch forward to pull prey into its throat. Up to 3m long and surprisingly intelligent.

潜伏在珊瑚礁缝隙中，海鳝拥有第二套颌骨——咽颌——可以向前弹出将猎物拉入喉咙。体长可达3米，且出奇聪明。

Double-Jawed / 双颌猎手

Shark vs. Myths: Setting the Record Straight / 鲨鱼与迷思：还原真相

Hollywood's Jaws cemented shark fear in popular culture — but the reality is far more nuanced.
好莱坞的《大白鲨》在流行文化中固化了人们对鲨鱼的恐惧——但现实远比电影复杂得多。

MYTH: Sharks are mindless killing machines / 迷思：鲨鱼是无脑的杀戮机器
FACT: Highly intelligent with complex behaviors / 事实：高度智能，行为复杂
Sharks exhibit curiosity, social hierarchies, and problem-solving abilities. Some species have brain-to-body ratios comparable to birds and mammals. / 鲨鱼表现出好奇心、社会等级和解决问题的能力。某些物种的脑体比堪比鸟类和哺乳动物。
MYTH: Sharks intentionally hunt humans / 迷思：鲨鱼故意猎杀人类
FACT: Humans are not natural prey / 事实：人类不是其天然猎物
Most bites are exploratory — sharks investigate with their mouths. Over 80% of victims survive, and fatal attacks average fewer than 10 per year globally. / 大多数咬伤是探索性的——鲨鱼用嘴来探查。超过80%的受害者存活，全球每年致命攻击平均不到10起。
MYTH: All sharks are dangerous / 迷思：所有鲨鱼都很危险
FACT: 500+ species, only ~12 involved in bites / 事实：500+物种，仅约12种涉及咬人事件
Whale sharks, basking sharks, and megamouth sharks are filter feeders. Most species are smaller than a human and pose no threat. / 鲸鲨、姥鲨和巨口鲨都是滤食动物。大多数物种体型比人类小，不构成威胁。
MYTH: Sharks are abundant and resilient / 迷思：鲨鱼数量众多且适应力强
FACT: 100 million killed annually by humans / 事实：每年被人类杀死1亿条
Overfishing, bycatch, and shark finning have driven many populations to decline by over 70%. Over one-third of shark species face extinction. / 过度捕捞、副渔获物和割取鱼翅已导致许多种群数量下降超过70%。超过三分之一的鲨鱼物种面临灭绝。
MYTH: Shark cartilage cures cancer / 迷思：鲨鱼软骨可以治愈癌症
FACT: No scientific evidence / 事实：没有科学证据
Extensive studies have found no anti-cancer properties in shark cartilage. This myth has driven unsustainable harvest of shark populations worldwide. / 大量研究未发现鲨鱼软骨具有抗癌特性。这一迷思在全球范围内推动了对鲨鱼种群不可持续的捕捞。
MYTH: Sharks don't get cancer / 迷思：鲨鱼不会得癌症
FACT: Sharks do develop cancer / 事实：鲨鱼确实会得癌症
Documented cases of tumors in sharks disprove this myth. The misconception stems from a 1970s marketing claim with no basis in marine biology. / 有记录的鲨鱼肿瘤案例已证明这一说法不实。这一误解源于1970年代一项毫无海洋生物学依据的营销说辞。

Shark Conservation Status / 鲨鱼保护现状

Shark Finning Crisis / 割取鱼翅危机

An estimated 73 million sharks are killed annually for their fins alone. Fins are often removed while the shark is still alive — the body discarded overboard. Finning is banned in many countries but enforcement remains inadequate.

每年估计有7300万条鲨鱼仅仅因为鱼翅而被杀害。鱼翅通常在鲨鱼还活着时被割下——尸体被抛弃入海。许多国家已禁止割取鱼翅，但执法力度仍然不足。

73M killed/year / 年杀7300万条 70% population decline since 1970 / 自1970年种群下降70% 37% species threatened / 37%物种受威胁 Apex predator removal cascades ecosystems / 顶级掠食者消失引发生态系统连锁反应

Removing sharks — the ocean's top predators — triggers trophic cascades: mid-level predators multiply and deplete herbivorous fish, which in turn allows algae to smother coral reefs. Healthy shark populations are a hallmark of a thriving ocean ecosystem.

移除鲨鱼——海洋的顶级掠食者——会引发营养级联效应：中层掠食者大量繁殖并耗尽植食性鱼类，进而导致藻类覆盖珊瑚礁。健康的鲨鱼种群是繁荣海洋生态系统的标志。

Hunting Strategy Comparison / 狩猎策略对比

Ambush Predation / 伏击捕食

Strategy / 策略: Remaining motionless, blending with the environment, then launching a surprise attack. / 保持不动，融入环境，然后发动突然袭击。

Practitioners / 实践者: Great white shark (vertical breach), wobbegong (camouflage), anglerfish (lure). / 大白鲨（垂直跃出攻击）、须鲨（伪装）、鮟鱇鱼（诱饵）。

Energy Cost / 能量消耗: Low. Relies on explosiveness over endurance. / 低。依靠爆发力而非耐力。

Success Rate / 成功率: ~50% for great white breaches. / 大白鲨跃出攻击约50%。

Pursuit Predation / 追击捕食

Strategy / 策略: Open-water chasing using superior speed and endurance to wear down prey. / 利用速度和耐力在开阔水域追击以消耗猎物。

Practitioners / 实践者: Mako shark (fastest shark), tuna, orca pods (coordinated pursuit). / 鲭鲨（最快的鲨鱼）、金枪鱼、虎鲸群（协同追击）。

Energy Cost / 能量消耗: High. Requires endothermic adaptations for sustained speed. / 高。需要内温性适应以维持持续速度。

Success Rate / 成功率: ~80% for orca pods hunting cooperatively. / 虎鲸群协同捕猎约80%。

Cooperative Hunting / 协同狩猎

Strategy / 策略: Coordinated group tactics to herd, confuse, or exhaust prey. / 协调群体战术围拢、迷惑或耗尽猎物。

Practitioners / 实践者: Orca pods (wave washing, carousel feeding), some shark species. / 虎鲸群（波浪冲刷、旋转捕食）、部分鲨鱼物种。

Energy Cost / 能量消耗: Shared among group members. / 群体成员分担。

Success Rate / 成功率: Highest — orca pods succeed on ~80%+ of coordinated hunts. / 最高——虎鲸群协同狩猎成功率约80%以上。

Electroreception / 电感受

Ampullae of Lorenzini detect electrical fields as weak as 5 nV/cm. All muscle contractions — even a fish hiding in sand — produce detectable electrical signals. This is every shark's "sixth sense."

洛伦兹壶腹可探测低至5纳伏/厘米的电场。所有肌肉收缩——即使是藏在沙中的鱼——都会产生可探测的电信号。这是每条鲨鱼的"第六感"。

👂

Hearing / 听觉

Sharks detect low-frequency sounds (10-800 Hz) from over 1 km away — especially the irregular vibrations of struggling prey. The inner ear also functions as an accelerometer for balance.

鲨鱼可从1公里以外探测低频声音（10-800赫兹）——尤其是挣扎猎物发出的不规则振动。内耳还作为加速度计维持平衡。

👁

Vision / 视觉

A tapetum lucidum reflective layer behind the retina gives sharks excellent low-light vision — 10x more sensitive than human eyes. Some species see color; most detect contrast exceptionally well.

视网膜后的照膜反射层赋予鲨鱼卓越的低光视力——比人眼敏感10倍。部分物种能看到颜色；大多数对对比度有极佳的识别能力。

👅

Olfaction (Smell) / 嗅觉

Sharks detect 1 part per million of blood in seawater — that is one drop in an Olympic swimming pool. However, the "blood frenzy" myth is exaggerated; sharks are attracted by many scents.

鲨鱼可探测海水中百万分之一的血液浓度——相当于奥运标准泳池中的一滴血。然而，"嗜血狂鲨"的传说被夸大了；鲨鱼会被多种气味吸引。

🌊

Lateral Line / 侧线

A canal system running along the body detects water displacement and vibrations. This "touch at a distance" sense allows navigation and prey detection in total darkness.

沿身体延伸的管道系统可探测水流位移和振动。这种"远程触觉"使其在完全黑暗中也能导航和探测猎物。
