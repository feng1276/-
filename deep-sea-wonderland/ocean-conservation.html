🪸 珊瑚礁修复项目

Coral Reef Restoration — Bringing Reefs Back to Life / 珊瑚礁修复——让礁石重获新生

Coral Nurseries / 珊瑚育苗场

在水下苗圃培育珊瑚幼体，待其成长后移植到受损礁区。全球已有超过 200+ 个活跃的珊瑚育苗项目。
Coral larvae are cultivated in underwater nurseries, then transplanted to damaged reefs. Over 200+ active projects worldwide.

Microfragmentation / 微碎裂技术

将珊瑚断枝固定在特制框架上，利用珊瑚无性繁殖能力快速扩增。生长速率可达自然环境的 25-40倍。
Coral fragments are secured on specialized frames, leveraging asexual reproduction for rapid expansion — growing 25-40x faster than in nature.

Heat-Resistant Coral / 耐热珊瑚培育

科学家通过筛选和培育耐热性珊瑚品种，帮助珊瑚礁适应逐渐变暖的海洋环境。这些"超级珊瑚"有望在 +2°C 的水温中生存。
Scientists breed heat-tolerant coral varieties to help reefs adapt to warming oceans. These "super corals" may survive at +2°C above normal.

🧪 气候变化与海洋酸化

Climate Change & Ocean Acidification — The Other CO2 Problem / 气候变化与海洋酸化——另一个CO2问题

How Acidification Works / 海洋酸化原理

The ocean absorbs about 30% of human CO2 emissions. When CO2 dissolves in seawater, it forms carbonic acid, lowering pH. Since the Industrial Revolution, ocean surface acidity has increased by about 30%. / 海洋吸收了人类排放的约 30% 的 CO2。当 CO2 溶于海水时，形成碳酸，降低海水 pH 值。自工业革命以来，海洋表层海水酸度已经增加了约 30%。

🤖 海洋清理科技

Ocean Cleanup Technologies — Innovation for a Cleaner Ocean / 海洋清理科技——创新驱动清洁海洋

The Ocean Cleanup / 海洋清理计划

U型浮动屏障系统，利用洋流自然收集塑料垃圾。目标：清除太平洋垃圾带 90% 的漂浮塑料。
U-shaped floating barrier system that uses ocean currents to naturally collect plastic. Goal: remove 90% of floating plastic from the Great Pacific Garbage Patch.

River Interceptors / 河流拦截器

部署在污染最严重的河流入海口，在塑料进入海洋之前进行拦截。已有 20+ 条河流部署了拦截装置。
Deployed at the mouths of the most polluted rivers, intercepting plastic before it reaches the ocean. 20+ rivers already equipped.

Seabin / 海洋垃圾桶

港口和码头安装的浮动垃圾桶，每天可收集约 1.5 kg 海洋垃圾，包括微塑料和油污。
Floating trash bins installed in harbors and marinas, collecting ~1.5 kg of marine debris daily, including microplastics and oil.

AI Autonomous Cleaners / AI 自主清理船

搭载 AI 视觉系统的自主航行船只，可识别和收集水面垃圾，同时避免误捕海洋生物。全天候作业。
Autonomous vessels with AI vision systems identify and collect surface debris while avoiding marine life bycatch. Operates 24/7.

🌟 成功案例 — 物种恢复的故事

Success Stories — Species Recovery Against the Odds / 成功案例——逆境中的物种复苏

Humpback Whale / 座头鲸

商业捕鲸使座头鲸数量锐减至不足 5,000 头。1986 年国际捕鲸禁令后，全球种群已恢复至约 84,000 头。
Commercial whaling reduced humpbacks to fewer than 5,000. After the 1986 moratorium, populations recovered to ~84,000.

📈 Recovered to 93% of historic levels / 恢复至 93% 历史水平

Sea Otter / 海獭

毛皮贸易使海獭从约 300,000 只锐减至约 1,000 只。1911 年保护令后，种群恢复至约 125,000 只。
The fur trade decimated sea otters from ~300,000 to ~1,000. After 1911 protection, populations recovered to ~125,000.

📈 Keystone species recovered / 关键种成功恢复

Green Sea Turtle / 绿海龟

筑巢地保护和禁捕措施，使多个绿海龟种群出现 显著恢复。夏威夷种群年增长率达 5.7%。
Nesting site protection and fishing bans have driven significant recovery across multiple populations. Hawaii's population growing at 5.7% annually.

📈 Multi-region population growth / 多地区种群增长
