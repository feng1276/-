Where the Ocean Drifts in Light / 海洋光影漂浮之处

Journey into the ethereal world of jellyfish, comb jellies, and gelatinous drifters — ancient creatures that have graced our oceans for over 500 million years, long before the first dinosaurs walked the Earth.

踏入水母、栉水母和胶质漂浮生物的缥缈世界——这些古老的生灵在地球上最早的恐龙出现之前，就已经在海洋中优雅生存了5亿多年。

The Jellyfish Life Cycle / 水母生命周期

Jellyfish undergo one of nature's most fascinating transformations — alternating between a stationary polyp phase and a free-swimming medusa phase.
水母经历了自然界最迷人的转变之一——在固着的水螅体阶段和自由游动的水母体阶段之间轮换。

Immortal Jellyfish Exception (Turritopsis dohrnii) / 永生水母例外（灯塔水母）

The immortal jellyfish can reverse its life cycle — transforming from medusa back into a polyp under stress. This biological immortality is unique among known animals and is the subject of intense scientific study for potential applications in regenerative medicine.

永生水母可以逆转其生命周期——在压力下从水母体转回水螅体。这种生物永生能力在已知动物中独一无二，现已成为再生医学潜在应用领域的研究热点。

Species Showcase / 物种展示

Moon Jellyfish / 海月水母

The most recognizable jellyfish. Translucent with four horseshoe-shaped gonads visible through the bell. Common in aquariums worldwide; mild sting is harmless to humans.

最具辨识度的水母。半透明，透过伞体可看到四个马蹄形的生殖腺。全球水族馆常见；轻微螫刺对人类无害。

Common Beauty / 常见之美

Lion's Mane Jellyfish / 狮鬃水母

The largest known jellyfish — its bell reaches 2.3m across with tentacles trailing over 36m, longer than a blue whale. Found in cold northern waters. Arthur Conan Doyle featured it in a Sherlock Holmes story.

已知最大的水母——伞体直径可达2.3米，触手延伸超过36米，比蓝鲸还长。分布于寒冷的北部海域。柯南·道尔将其写入了福尔摩斯故事。

Giant of the Deep / 深海巨兽

Box Jellyfish / 箱水母

One of the most venomous creatures on Earth — its sting can cause cardiac arrest within minutes. Uniquely, it has 24 eyes (of 4 different types) and actively hunts rather than drifting passively.

地球上最毒的动物之一——其螫刺可在数分钟内导致心脏骤停。独特之处在于它有24只眼睛（4种不同类型），且主动捕猎而非被动漂浮。

Venomous Hunter / 剧毒猎手

Portuguese Man o' War / 僧帽水母

Not a true jellyfish but a colonial organism composed of specialized zooids working together. Its gas-filled float acts as a sail, and its tentacles can stretch 30m. Extremely painful sting.

并非真正的水母，而是一个由特化个体协同工作的群体生物。其充气浮囊如同船帆，触手可延伸30米。螫刺极其疼痛。

Colonial Drifter / 群体漂流者

Immortal Jellyfish / 永生水母

Just 4.5mm across, this tiny jellyfish can revert its cells to an earlier developmental stage — essentially resetting its life. Biological immortality in a creature the size of a fingernail.

直径仅4.5毫米，这种微小水母可将其细胞逆转到更早的发育阶段——本质上重置其生命。在一个指甲盖大小的生物中实现了生物永生。

The Chemistry of Living Light / 生命之光的化学反应

How Jellyfish Create Light / 水母如何创造光

Bioluminescence is a chemical reaction that produces "cold light" — nearly 100% efficient with almost no heat. The process involves two key molecules:

生物发光是一种产生"冷光"的化学反应——效率接近100%，几乎不产生热量。这个过程涉及两种关键分子：

Luciferin / 荧光素 — the light-producing pigment / 产生光的色素

A small molecule that emits light when oxidized. / 一种氧化时会发光的小分子。

Luciferase / Photoprotein / 荧光素酶/发光蛋白 — the catalyst enzyme / 催化剂酶

Speeds up the oxidation reaction. In jellyfish, the photoprotein aequorin (discovered in Aequorea victoria) reacts with calcium ions to emit blue-green light. / 加速氧化反应。在水母中，发光蛋白aequorin（发现于维多利亚多管发光水母）与钙离子反应，发出蓝绿色光。

GFP (Green Fluorescent Protein) / 绿色荧光蛋白: Another jellyfish discovery — this protein absorbs blue bioluminescent light and re-emits it as green. GFP revolutionized cell biology, earning a Nobel Prize in Chemistry in 2008.

另一项水母发现——这种蛋白吸收蓝色生物发光并将其重新发射为绿色。GFP彻底改变了细胞生物学，并于2008年获得诺贝尔化学奖。

Jellyfish Swarms & Climate Change / 水母潮群与气候变化

Jellyfish populations are booming in many parts of the world, forming massive swarms (blooms) that can cover hundreds of square kilometers. These blooms are increasingly linked to human-driven environmental changes.

水母种群正在全球许多地区激增，形成可覆盖数百平方公里的大规模潮群（水华）。这些爆发日益与人类驱动的环境变化相关联。

Why Are Jellyfish Increasing? / 水母为何在增加？

Warmer Waters / 水温升高: Higher temperatures accelerate jellyfish metabolism and reproduction. / 更高温度加速水母代谢和繁殖。
Overfishing / 过度捕捞: Removing fish competitors and predators leaves more resources for jellyfish. / 移除鱼类竞争者和掠食者为水母留下更多资源。
Eutrophication / 富营养化: Nutrient runoff creates low-oxygen dead zones where jellyfish thrive but fish cannot. / 营养物流失造成低氧死区，水母可以繁盛但鱼类不能。
Ocean Acidification / 海洋酸化: Jellyfish are more tolerant than calcifying organisms like corals and shellfish. / 水母比珊瑚和贝类等钙化生物更具耐受性。

Ecosystem Impact / 生态系统影响: Massive swarms can clog fishing nets, shut down nuclear power plant cooling intakes, and disrupt marine food webs. In some regions, "jellyfish-dominated" ecosystems are replacing fish-dominated ones — a phenomenon known as a regime shift.

大规模潮群可堵塞渔网、关闭核电站冷却水取水口、破坏海洋食物网。在一些地区，"水母主导"的生态系统正在取代"鱼类主导"的生态系统——这一现象被称为体制转变。

Comb Jellies: Nature's Rainbow Machines / 栉水母：大自然的彩虹机器

Ctenophora — not true jellyfish but a distinct phylum / 栉水母动物门——不是真正的水母，而是一个独立的门

Iridescence, Not Bioluminescence / 虹彩而非生物发光

Comb jellies (ctenophores) produce their mesmerizing rainbow displays through light diffraction, not bioluminescence. Their eight "comb rows" are lined with thousands of cilia that beat in synchronized waves.

栉水母通过光衍射而非生物发光产生迷人的彩虹展示。它们的八列"栉板"上排列着数以千计以同步波形式摆动的纤毛。

As light passes through these beating cilia, it is diffracted into its spectral components — much like light passing through a prism or a CD. The colors shift and dance as the cilia beat rhythmically.

当光线穿过这些摆动的纤毛时，被衍射成光谱成分——如同光线穿过棱镜或光盘。随着纤毛有节奏地摆动，色彩变幻舞动。

Fun Fact / 趣闻: Comb jellies are possibly the oldest animal lineage on Earth. Genetic studies suggest they may have branched off before sponges — making them the sister group to ALL other animals. / 栉水母可能是地球上最古老的动物谱系。基因研究表明它们可能在海绵之前就已分支——使它们成为所有其他动物的姐妹群。

Jellyfish in Medicine & Research / 水母在医学与研究中的应用

Jellyfish are not only mesmerizing creatures — they have revolutionized biology and medicine. From glowing proteins to potential cancer treatments, these ancient animals are biomedical treasures.
水母不仅是令人着迷的生物——它们还彻底改变了生物学和医学。从荧光蛋白到潜在的癌症治疗方法，这些古老的动物是生物医学的宝藏。

🏆 GFP: The Nobel Protein / GFP：诺贝尔奖蛋白

Green Fluorescent Protein (GFP), discovered in the crystal jelly Aequorea victoria, earned the 2008 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. Scientists fuse GFP to other proteins to track gene expression, watch cancer cells spread, and study brain connections in real time.

绿色荧光蛋白（GFP）发现于维多利亚多管发光水母，获得了2008年诺贝尔化学奖。科学家将GFP与其它蛋白融合，用于追踪基因表达、观察癌细胞扩散、实时研究大脑连接。

💊 Venom as Medicine / 毒液入药

Box jellyfish venom contains potent compounds being studied for targeted drug delivery. Specific peptides in their venom selectively bind to cancer cells, opening possibilities for precision cancer treatments.

箱水母毒液含有强效化合物，正在研究用于靶向药物递送。其毒液中的特定肽可选择性地与癌细胞结合，为精准癌症治疗开辟了可能。

🨀 Collagen & Wound Healing / 胶原蛋白与伤口愈合

Jellyfish collagen is remarkably similar to human collagen and is being developed for wound dressings, skin grafts, and anti-aging treatments. It's hypoallergenic and can be harvested sustainably without harming ecosystems.

水母胶原蛋白与人类胶原蛋白非常相似，正被开发用于伤口敷料、皮肤移植和抗衰老治疗。它低过敏性，可可持续采收而不损害生态系统。

🔬 Brain Research / 大脑研究

The diffuse nerve net of jellyfish provides insights into the evolution of nervous systems. Studying how jellyfish coordinate movement without a centralized brain is inspiring new approaches to distributed computing and AI.

水母的弥散神经网为理解神经系统的进化提供了洞见。研究水母如何在缺乏集中大脑的情况下协调运动，正启发分布式计算和人工智能的新方法。

Sting Treatment & Safety Guide / 螫伤处理与安全指南

⚠ What NOT to Do / 切勿做的

  • ❌ Do NOT rinse with fresh water — it triggers nematocysts to fire / 切勿用淡水冲洗——会触发刺细胞发射
  • ❌ Do NOT apply vinegar to box jellyfish stings (vinegar works for most, but worsens box jelly stings) / 切勿对箱水母螫伤使用醋（醋对大多数水母有效，但会加重箱水母螫伤）
  • ❌ Do NOT rub the area — pressure triggers unfired stinging cells / 切勿摩擦伤口——压力会触发未发射的刺细胞
  • ❌ Do NOT apply urine, alcohol, or ammonia — popular myths that make stings worse / 切勿使用尿液、酒精或氨水——这些常见的说法反而会加重螫伤
  • ❌ Do NOT apply pressure bandages — they concentrate venom / 切勿施加压力绷带——会集中毒液
  • ❌ Do NOT scrape with a credit card before deactivating nematocysts / 在灭活刺细胞之前切勿用信用卡刮擦

✅ Correct Treatment Steps / 正确处理步骤

  1. Rinse immediately with seawater (not fresh water) / 立即用海水冲洗（非淡水）
  2. For most jellyfish: apply vinegar (5% acetic acid) for at least 30 seconds / 对大多数水母：用醋（5%醋酸）冲洗至少30秒
  3. Remove visible tentacles with tweezers (protected hands) / 用镊子移除可见触手（保护好双手）
  4. Immerse in hot water (45°C / 113°F) for 20-45 minutes / 浸泡在45°C热水中20-45分钟
  5. Take antihistamines for itching and swelling / 服用抗组胺药缓解瘙痒和肿胀
  6. Seek emergency care for box jellyfish stings, severe pain, or difficulty breathing / 对箱水母螫伤、剧烈疼痛或呼吸困难应寻求紧急医疗
  7. CPR may be needed for box jellyfish — venom can cause cardiac arrest within minutes / 箱水母螫伤可能需要进行心肺复苏——毒液可在数分钟内导致心脏骤停

🌊 Prevention Tips / 预防提示

Swim at patrolled beaches. Wear protective clothing (rash guards, wetsuits). Check local reports for jellyfish warnings. Avoid swimming after heavy rain when jellies wash closer to shore. / 在有巡逻的海滩游泳。穿着防护服。查看当地水母预警报告。大雨后水母冲近岸边时避免下水。

🏥 When to Call Emergency / 何时拨打急救

Call emergency services immediately for: box jellyfish stings, difficulty breathing, chest pain, severe swelling, muscle cramps, nausea, or if the sting covers a large area of the body. / 出现以下情况立即呼叫急救：箱水母螫伤、呼吸困难、胸痛、严重肿胀、肌肉痉挛、恶心，或螫伤面积覆盖身体大面积。

🔬 Deadliest Species / 最致命物种

The box jellyfish (Chironex fleckeri) is the most venomous marine animal. Its venom can kill a human in under 5 minutes. Fortunately, antivenom exists and fatalities are rare with prompt treatment. / 箱水母（Chironex fleckeri）是最毒的海生动物。其毒液可在5分钟内致人死亡。所幸已有抗毒血清，及时治疗下死亡案例极少。
