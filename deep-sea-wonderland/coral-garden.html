Coral Garden / 珊瑚花园

Dive into a kaleidoscope of corals, where every branch and polyp pulses with life. The rainforest of the sea awaits.
潜入珊瑚的万花筒世界，每一根枝条和每一个珊瑚虫都充满生命的气息。海洋中的热带雨林正等待着您。

Diversity of Corals / 珊瑚的多样性

Brain Coral / 脑珊瑚

Massive, dome-shaped colonies with meandering ridges resembling a brain. Can live for over 900 years, making them some of the oldest living organisms on Earth.
巨大的圆顶形珊瑚群体，表面有类似大脑回旋的脊状纹路。可存活900年以上，是地球上最古老的生物之一。

Staghorn Coral / 鹿角珊瑚

Fast-growing branching coral crucial to reef building. Its antler-like branches provide shelter for countless reef fish and grow up to 10 cm per year.
快速生长的分枝状珊瑚，对礁石形成至关重要。其鹿角般的枝杈为无数珊瑚礁鱼类提供庇护，每年可生长高达10厘米。

Sea Fan (Gorgonian) / 海扇（柳珊瑚）

Delicate, fan-shaped colonies that orient perpendicular to water currents to maximize filter feeding. Composed of flexible protein called gorgonin.
精致的扇形群体，垂直于水流方向生长以最大化滤食效率。由名为柳珊瑚素的柔韧蛋白质构成。

Table Coral / 桌状珊瑚

Flat, table-like formations that shade out competitors below. A single table coral can span over 3 meters and is home to hundreds of small fish.
扁平的桌面状结构，为下方的竞争者提供遮荫。一株桌状珊瑚可跨越3米以上，成为数百条小鱼的栖息地。

Symbiosis: Clownfish & Anemone / 共生：小丑鱼与海葵

A Perfect Partnership / 完美的伙伴关系

The clownfish and sea anemone share one of nature's most beautiful mutualisms. The anemone's stinging tentacles protect the clownfish from predators, while the clownfish chases away anemone-eating fish like butterflyfish.
小丑鱼和海葵共享自然界中最美丽的互利共生关系之一。海葵带刺的触手保护小丑鱼免受捕食者的侵害，而小丑鱼则赶走蝴蝶鱼等以海葵为食的鱼类。

Clownfish develop immunity to the anemone's sting by gradually coating themselves in the anemone's mucus. In return, the clownfish's waste provides nitrogen that helps the anemone's symbiotic algae grow.
小丑鱼通过逐渐用海葵的黏液覆盖自己来获得对海葵刺细胞的免疫力。作为回报，小丑鱼的排泄物提供的氮元素有助于海葵的共生藻类生长。

This relationship demonstrates mutualism / 互利共生 — both species benefit. The clownfish also aerates the anemone and removes parasites from its tentacles.
这种关系展示了互利共生——双方都受益。小丑鱼还为海葵增加氧气并清除触手上的寄生虫。

Reef Cleanup Crew / 礁石清理队

Parrotfish: The Sand Makers / 鹦鹉鱼：造沙者

Parrotfish use their beak-like fused teeth to scrape algae off dead coral. A single large parrotfish can produce up to 90 kg of sand per year / 每年90公斤沙子 — most of the white sand on tropical beaches comes from parrotfish digestion!
鹦鹉鱼用它们喙状的融合牙齿从死珊瑚上刮食藻类。一条大型鹦鹉鱼每年可产生高达90公斤的沙子——热带海滩上大部分白色沙子都来自鹦鹉鱼的消化！

By controlling algae growth, parrotfish prevent algae from smothering live coral. On healthy reefs, parrotfish are the primary grazers that keep the ecosystem in balance.
通过控制藻类生长，鹦鹉鱼防止藻类覆盖活珊瑚。在健康的珊瑚礁上，鹦鹉鱼是维持生态系统平衡的主要食草者。

At night, some species wrap themselves in a mucus cocoon to mask their scent from predators like moray eels.
夜间，一些物种会用黏液茧包裹自己，以掩盖气味，躲避海鳝等捕食者。

⚠ Coral Bleaching: A Global Crisis / 珊瑚白化：全球危机

When ocean temperatures rise just 1–2 degrees Celsius above normal, corals expel their symbiotic zooxanthellae algae, turning ghostly white. Without these algae, the coral starves. Since 2016, the Great Barrier Reef has experienced four mass bleaching events.
当海洋温度仅比正常温度高1-2摄氏度时，珊瑚会排出共生的虫黄藻，变成惨白色。没有这些藻类，珊瑚会饿死。自2016年以来，大堡礁已遭受四次大规模白化事件。

Over 50% of the world's coral reefs have been lost since 1950. / 自1950年以来，全球超过50%的珊瑚礁已经消失。 Scientists estimate that without significant climate action, 90% of coral reefs could disappear by 2050. / 科学家估计，如果不采取重大气候行动，到2050年90%的珊瑚礁可能消失。

Bleaching severity index — each bar represents a year of increasing ocean temperatures (2016–2024) / 白化严重程度指数——每个柱状代表一年海洋温度升高的程度（2016-2024年）

The Reef at Night / 夜幕下的珊瑚礁

When the sun sets, the reef transforms. Nocturnal hunters like squirrelfish and cardinalfish emerge. Parrotfish wrap themselves in mucus sleeping bags. Basket stars unfurl their intricate arms. Moray eels slip through the coral in search of prey.
当太阳落山时，珊瑚礁焕然一新。夜行性猎手如锯鳞鱼和天竺鲷开始出没。鹦鹉鱼用黏液睡袋包裹自己。篮星展开它们复杂的腕臂。海鳝在珊瑚间穿行寻找猎物。

Bioluminescent organisms begin to glow, and the reef's color palette shifts from vibrant daylight hues to mysterious blues and greens.
生物发光生物开始发光，珊瑚礁的色彩从生动的日光色调转变为神秘的蓝绿色调。

Species Spotlight / 物种聚焦

Moorish Idol / 镰鱼

Zanclus cornutus

Iconic reef fish with a long dorsal filament and bold black-and-yellow bands. Solitary or in pairs, they feed on sponges and tunicates.
标志性珊瑚鱼，背部有长条状鳍刺和醒目的黑黄色条纹。独居或成对出现，以海绵和海鞘为食。

Magnificent Anemone / 华丽海葵

Heteractis magnifica

Hosts 14 species of clownfish. Its tentacles contain stinging nematocysts that deter most fish but welcome symbiotic partners.
寄居14种小丑鱼。其触手含有刺细胞，可驱退大多数鱼类，但欢迎共生伙伴。

Leafy Seadragon / 叶海龙

Phycodurus eques

Master of disguise among kelp and seagrass. Its leaf-like appendages provide perfect camouflage. Found only in southern Australian waters.
海藻和海草间的伪装大师。其叶片状附肢提供完美的伪装。仅存在于澳大利亚南部水域。

Coral Spawning Spectacle / 珊瑚产卵奇观

Once a year, corals synchronize one of nature's most breathtaking events — releasing millions of egg and sperm bundles into the water column in a coordinated mass spawning.
每年一次，珊瑚会同步自然界最令人叹为观止的事件之一——在协调一致的大规模产卵中释放数百万个卵子和精子束进入水体。

🌕 Lunar Trigger / 月球触发

Corals use multiple environmental cues to synchronize: water temperature rise, lunar phase (full moon), and time of sunset. This ensures that colonies across hundreds of kilometers spawn within the same few nights.
珊瑚利用多种环境信号同步：水温升高、月相（满月）和日落时间。这确保了数百公里范围内的珊瑚群落在相同的几个夜晚内产卵。

⏱ Split-Second Timing / 精确到秒的时间

Individual coral colonies release their gametes within a 20-minute window. The egg-sperm bundles float to the surface where fertilization occurs, forming coral larvae called planulae.
单个珊瑚群体在20分钟的时间窗口内释放配子。卵精束浮到水面后在水中受精，形成名为浮浪幼虫的珊瑚幼体。

🧬 Genetic Mixing / 基因混合

Mass spawning maximizes genetic diversity by mixing gametes from thousands of colonies. This genetic recombination is essential for reef resilience against disease and environmental change.
大规模产卵通过混合数千个群体的配子来最大化遗传多样性。这种基因重组对于珊瑚礁抵御疾病和环境变化的适应力至关重要。

🔬 Larval Journey / 幼虫之旅

Planulae drift with ocean currents for days to weeks before settling on suitable substrate. Only about 1 in 10,000 survives to become a mature coral colony — making each survivor precious.
浮浪幼虫随洋流漂流数天到数周，然后在合适的基质上定居。只有大约万分之一能存活成为成熟的珊瑚群体——使每一个幸存者都弥足珍贵。

Reef Restoration Projects / 珊瑚礁修复项目

Around the world, scientists and communities are pioneering innovative techniques to restore damaged reefs. Here are the most promising approaches being deployed today.
全球各地的科学家和社区正在开创创新技术来修复受损的珊瑚礁。以下是当今正在部署的最有前景的方法。

🏗 Coral Gardening / 珊瑚园艺

Small coral fragments are grown in underwater nurseries — rope lines or table structures. After 6-12 months, healthy colonies are transplanted onto degraded reefs. Over 10,000 corals are outplanted annually in the Caribbean alone.
小型珊瑚碎片在水下苗圃中培育——绳索或桌式结构。6-12个月后，健康的群体被移植到退化珊瑚礁上。仅在加勒比地区，每年就外植超过10,000株珊瑚。

⚡ Mineral Accretion / 矿物沉积

Low-voltage electricity passed through steel structures in seawater causes calcium carbonate to precipitate. Corals grown on these structures grow 3-5x faster and show up to 50% higher survival rates after bleaching events.
低电压电流通过海水中的钢结构导致碳酸钙沉淀。在这些结构上生长的珊瑚生长速度快3-5倍，白化事件后的存活率高出50%。

🧪 Assisted Evolution / 辅助进化

Scientists identify naturally heat-tolerant coral colonies and selectively breed them. The offspring can withstand temperatures 1.5-2°C higher than average — a crucial adaptation in warming oceans.
科学家识别自然耐热的珊瑚群体并选择性繁殖。后代可以耐受比平均温度高1.5-2°C的温度——这是变暖海洋中的关键适应。

🤖 Robot Reef Workers / 机器人珊瑚礁工作者

Autonomous underwater robots like RangerBot and LarvalBot disperse coral larvae over damaged reefs. A single robot can cover 3 hectares per hour — 100x faster than manual diver deployment.
RangerBot和LarvalBot等自主水下机器人在受损珊瑚礁上播撒珊瑚幼虫。一台机器人每小时可覆盖3公顷——比人工潜水部署快100倍。
