Turtle Sanctuary / 海龟庇护所

Ancient Mariners of the Blue Planet / 蓝色星球的古老航海者

Sea turtles have navigated Earth's oceans for over 110 million years — surviving mass extinctions that claimed the dinosaurs. Today, all seven species face unprecedented threats. Welcome to their sanctuary.

海龟在地球海洋中航行了超过1.1亿年——经历了使恐龙灭绝的大规模灭绝事件幸存至今。如今，全部七个物种都面临前所未有的威胁。欢迎来到它们的庇护所。

Meet the Sea Turtles / 认识海龟

Green Sea Turtle / 绿海龟

Named for the green color of their body fat from a seagrass diet. The only herbivorous sea turtle as an adult. Found in tropical and subtropical waters worldwide. Can hold breath for up to 5 hours while sleeping.

因其食海草而呈绿色的体脂得名。成年后是唯一植食性的海龟。分布于全球热带和亚热带海域。睡觉时可屏息长达5小时。

Endangered / 濒危

Loggerhead Turtle / 蠵龟

Distinguished by its disproportionately large head and powerful jaws, evolved to crush hard-shelled prey like conchs and crabs. The most common sea turtle in U.S. waters. Exceptional navigators.

以其不成比例的大头和强壮的下颌著称，进化用于咬碎海螺和螃蟹等硬壳猎物。美国海域最常见的海龟。卓越的导航者。

Vulnerable / 易危

Leatherback Turtle / 棱皮龟

The largest sea turtle — up to 2.4m and 700kg. Lacks a hard shell; instead has leathery skin covering a mosaic of tiny bones. The deepest-diving reptile, reaching 1,200m. Feeds almost exclusively on jellyfish.

最大的海龟——体长可达2.4米，重达700公斤。没有硬壳；而是由革质皮肤覆盖着细密骨质镶嵌而成。潜深最大的爬行动物，可达1200米。几乎只以水母为食。

Vulnerable / 易危

Hawksbill Turtle / 玳瑁

Named for its sharp, curved beak — perfect for extracting sponges from coral crevices. Its stunning amber shell with intricate patterns made it a target of the tortoiseshell trade for centuries.

因其尖锐弯曲的喙而得名——非常适合从珊瑚缝隙中取食海绵。其美丽琥珀色龟壳上的精美图案使其成为数个世纪以来玳瑁贸易的目标。

Critically Endangered / 极危

Kemp's Ridley / 肯氏龟

The smallest and rarest sea turtle, with a nearly circular shell. Famous for synchronized mass nesting events called "arribadas" where thousands of females come ashore simultaneously.

最小最稀有的海龟，龟壳近乎圆形。以名为"arribadas"的同步大规模筑巢事件而闻名，届时数千只雌龟同时上岸。

Critically Endangered / 极危

The Epic Life Journey / 壮丽的生命旅程

A sea turtle's life is one of the ocean's greatest odysseys — from a sandy nest to thousands of miles of open ocean navigation.
海龟的生命是海洋中最伟大的奥德赛之一——从沙巢到数千英里的开阔大洋航行。

Nesting / 筑巢

Female returns to natal beach. Digs nest, lays 100+ eggs. Covers with sand. Returns to sea. / 雌龟返回出生地海滩。挖巢，产下100+枚蛋。用沙覆盖。返回大海。

Incubation / 孵化

45-70 days. Temperature determines sex — warmer sand = more females. Cooler = more males. / 45-70天。温度决定性别——沙温越高雌性越多。沙温越低雄性越多。

Hatchling Dash / 幼龟冲刺

Hatchlings emerge at night, race to ocean guided by moonlight. Only ~1 in 1,000 survive to adulthood. / 幼龟夜间出壳，在月光指引下奔向海洋。约千分之一存活至成年。

Lost Years / 迷失岁月

Drift in open ocean gyres for 5-10 years. Feed on plankton in floating sargassum mats. / 在开阔大洋环流中漂流5-10年。在漂浮的马尾藻垫中以浮游生物为食。

Juvenile / 幼年

Move to coastal feeding grounds. Grow rapidly. Develop adult diet preferences. / 移至沿海觅食场。快速生长。形成成年食性偏好。

Adult & Return / 成年与回归

Sexual maturity at 20-50 years. Migrate thousands of miles to natal beach to nest — completing the cycle. / 20-50岁性成熟。迁徙数千英里回到出生地海滩筑巢——完成循环。

Night on the Nesting Beach / 筑巢海滩之夜

Hatchlings use moonlight reflected on the ocean to find their way. Artificial lights disorient them. / 幼龟利用海面反射的月光来辨认方向。人造灯光会使其迷失方向。

The Miracle of Arribada / 阿里巴达奇迹

Kemp's ridley and olive ridley turtles are famous for arribadas — Spanish for "arrivals" — where thousands of females simultaneously come ashore to nest. This synchronized mass nesting is one of nature's most spectacular events.

肯氏龟和榄蠵龟以arribadas（西班牙语"抵达"）而闻名——数千只雌龟同时上岸筑巢。这种同步大规模筑巢是自然界最壮观的事件之一。

Female turtles return to the exact beach where they were born, navigating across thousands of miles of open ocean using Earth's magnetic field as a geomagnetic map. This phenomenon is called natal philopatry.

雌龟利用地球磁场作为地磁地图，穿越数千英里的开阔大洋，精确返回其出生的海滩。这一现象被称为出生地归巢性。

Temperature-Dependent Sex / 温度依赖性别的决定: The sex of sea turtle hatchlings is determined by sand temperature during incubation. Warmer nests produce females; cooler nests produce males. Climate change threatens to skew populations — in some regions, over 99% of green turtle hatchlings are female. / 海龟幼龟的性别由孵化期间沙温决定。温度较高产生雌性；温度较低产生雄性。气候变化正在威胁种群性别比例——在某些地区，超过99%的绿海龟幼龟为雌性。

Migration Routes: Ocean Nomads / 迁徙路线：海洋游牧者

Navigation Superpowers / 导航超能力

Sea turtles navigate across entire ocean basins using multiple sensory systems:
海龟利用多种感官系统穿越整个海洋流域航行：

Geomagnetic Imprinting / 地磁印记: Hatchlings imprint on their natal beach's unique magnetic signature — a combination of inclination angle and intensity that creates a "magnetic address." / 幼龟对其出生地海滩独特的地磁特征形成印记——由磁倾角和磁场强度的组合形成"磁地址"。

Celestial Navigation / 天文导航: Use wave direction and sunlight to orient. Hatchlings swim toward the brightest horizon — the open ocean. / 利用波浪方向和阳光定向。幼龟朝着最亮的地平线——开阔大洋游去。

Record Distances / 创纪录距离: Leatherbacks travel up to 16,000 km per year, crossing the entire Pacific from nesting beaches in Indonesia to feeding grounds off California. / 棱皮龟每年旅行可达16,000公里，从印尼筑巢海滩穿越整个太平洋到达加利福尼亚近海的觅食场。

Threats to Survival / 生存威胁

Plastic Pollution / 塑料污染

Over 50% of sea turtles have ingested plastic. Floating bags resemble jellyfish — a primary food source. Plastic causes intestinal blockage, malnutrition, and death. / 超过50%的海龟摄入过塑料。漂浮的塑料袋像水母——海龟的主要食物来源。塑料导致肠道堵塞、营养不良和死亡。

Bycatch / 副渔获物

250,000 sea turtles are accidentally caught in fishing gear annually. Longlines, trawls, and gillnets are primary culprits. TEDs (Turtle Excluder Devices) reduce bycatch by up to 97%. / 每年有250,000只海龟被意外捕获在渔具中。延绳钓、拖网和刺网是主要元凶。海龟排除装置（TED）可减少高达97%的副渔获。

Habitat Loss / 栖息地丧失

Coastal development destroys nesting beaches. Seagrass meadow degradation eliminates feeding grounds. 50% of coral reefs — critical hawksbill habitat — have been lost since 1950. / 沿海开发破坏了筑巢海滩。海草草甸退化消除了觅食场。自1950年以来，50%的珊瑚礁——玳瑁的关键栖息地——已经丧失。

Climate Change / 气候变化

Rising temperatures skew sex ratios dangerously female. Sea level rise inundates nesting beaches. Ocean acidification threatens food web stability. / 气温升高导致性别比例严重偏向雌性。海平面上升淹没筑巢海滩。海洋酸化威胁食物网稳定。

Marine Iguana: The Dragon of Galapagos / 海鬣蜥：加拉帕戈斯之龙

A Living Fossil / 活化石

The marine iguana is the world's only seagoing lizard, found exclusively in the Galapagos Islands. Charles Darwin described them as "hideous-looking" but "most harmless" creatures — a testament to evolution's creativity.

海鬣蜥是世界上唯一的海生蜥蜴，仅分布于加拉帕戈斯群岛。查尔斯·达尔文描述它们"外表丑陋"但"极为无害"——这正是进化创造力的见证。

Unique Adaptations / 独特适应: Flattened tail for swimming, blunt snout for scraping algae off rocks, and specialized nasal glands that expel excess salt through a dramatic "sneeze." / 扁平的尾巴用于游泳，钝吻用于从岩石上刮食藻类，特化的鼻腔腺体通过戏剧性的"打喷嚏"排出多余盐分。

Diving Ability / 潜水能力: Can hold breath for up to 60 minutes and dive to 30m. After cold dives, they bask on black volcanic rocks to raise body temperature — turning their dark skin into a natural solar panel. / 可屏息长达60分钟，潜水至30米。冷水潜水后，它们在黑色火山岩上晒太阳以升高体温——将深色皮肤变为天然太阳能板。

Threat Status / 威胁状态: Vulnerable. Threatened by El Nino events (which kill their algae food), introduced predators (cats, rats, dogs), and oil spills. / 易危。受厄尔尼诺事件（导致其藻类食物死亡）、外来掠食者（猫、鼠、狗）和石油泄漏的威胁。

Sea Snakes: Venomous Mariners / 海蛇：有毒的航海者

The Yellow-Bellied Sea Snake / 黄腹海蛇

Hydrophis platurus is the most widely distributed snake on Earth, drifting across the entire tropical Indo-Pacific. Its paddle-shaped tail and laterally compressed body make it an efficient swimmer capable of staying at sea for months.

长吻海蛇是地球上分布最广的蛇类，漂流跨越整个热带印度洋-太平洋区域。其桨状尾巴和侧扁的身体使其成为高效的游泳者，能持续在海上生活数月。

Venom / 毒液: Up to 10x more potent than a cobra's, but bites to humans are extremely rare — they are docile and have small fangs. Their venom is designed for rapid prey immobilization (small fish). / 毒性高达眼镜蛇的10倍，但咬人极其罕见——它们性情温和且毒牙很小。其毒液专为快速制服猎物（小型鱼类）而设计。

Unique Trait / 独特特征: Can absorb up to 25% of its oxygen through its skin, and eliminate CO2 the same way — a crucial adaptation for extended dives. / 可通过皮肤吸收高达25%的氧气，并以同样方式排出二氧化碳——这是延长潜水时间的关键适应。

How You Can Help Sea Turtles / 如何帮助海龟

🌿

Reduce Plastic / 减少塑料

Use reusable bags and bottles. Refuse single-use plastics that end up in the ocean where turtles mistake them for food. / 使用环保袋和可重复使用的瓶子。拒绝使用一次性塑料，它们最终流入海洋，被海龟误认为食物。

🌊

Keep Beaches Dark / 保持海滩黑暗

If you live near a nesting beach, turn off lights at night during nesting season. Hatchlings use moonlight to find the ocean. / 如果你住在筑巢海滩附近，在筑巢季节夜间关闭灯光。幼龟依赖月光找到大海。

🐟

Choose Sustainable Seafood / 选择可持续海鲜

Look for MSC certification. Avoid seafood caught without Turtle Excluder Devices or that contributes to bycatch. / 寻找MSC认证。避免购买未使用海龟排除装置或助长副渔获的海鲜产品。

🤝

Support Conservation / 支持保护

Donate to organizations like the Sea Turtle Conservancy. Volunteer for beach cleanups and nest monitoring programs. / 向海龟保护协会等组织捐款。志愿参与海滩清洁和巢穴监测项目。

✈

Travel Responsibly / 负责任旅行

Never disturb nesting turtles. Book tours with certified eco-operators. Do not buy tortoiseshell souvenirs (made from hawksbill shells). / 绝不打扰筑巢海龟。预订有认证的生态旅游机构。不购买玳瑁制品（由玳瑁龟壳制成）。

📖

Spread Awareness / 传播意识

Share what you've learned. Education is the first step toward changing behaviors that threaten sea turtle survival. / 分享你学到的知识。教育是改变威胁海龟生存行为的第一步。

Adopt a Turtle: Wear Their Journey / 领养一只海龟：佩戴它们的旅程

The Turtle Tracking Bracelet / 海龟追踪手环 program links you to a real, satellite-tagged sea turtle. Each bracelet comes with a unique ID that lets you follow your turtle's migration online. Proceeds support nesting beach protection and bycatch reduction programs.

海龟追踪手环计划将你与一只真实的、带有卫星标签的海龟连接起来。每个手环都有唯一ID，可让你在线追踪你的海龟的迁徙路线。收益用于支持筑巢海滩保护和副渔获减少项目。
